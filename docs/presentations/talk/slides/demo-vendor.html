Check a supplier's SBOM before you accept it

$ pixi sbom --from-sbom vendor.cdx.json --report quality

Quality: 77 of 100 for 24 packages

$ pixi sbom --from-sbom vendor.cdx.json --min-quality 80 --output -

Gate failed: quality (1). Exiting 10.

$ pixi sbom --lockfile examples/projects/uv/02-flask/uv.lock -p linux-64 --vulnerabilities osv --vex-in examples/lab/vendor.openvex.json --report vulnerabilities

Summary: 40 open findings in 8 packages, 1 ignored

Score what you were sent, gate on it, and take a supplier's "not affected" statements as VEX, with their reasons kept in the record.
