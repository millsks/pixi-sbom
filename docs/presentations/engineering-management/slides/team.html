Every gate is off by default. Leave them off and it writes the document and exits clean.

The exit codes are the part engineering management cares about: these are ordinary CI failures, and they're distinguishable, so a pipeline can treat a license violation differently from a compile error. Say clearly that the gates ship off. For the trial, leave all of them off, collect documents for two weeks, then look at what they would have caught. Nobody's build breaks while we're deciding.
