Know what's in what we ship

pixi-sbom writes a software bill of materials for every environment we build. One job in CI, and we can answer the question.

Start with the problem, not the tool. We build on hundreds of third-party packages and right now we can't produce a reliable list of them when someone asks. Say the ask is small and it's on the next slide, so nobody spends this one wondering what you want from them. Mixed room: the engineering side wants to know what it costs to run, the security side wants to know whether it's credible. Both get answered.
