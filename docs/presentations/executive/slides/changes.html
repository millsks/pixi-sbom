Same engineers, same release process. The answers stop being projects.

Walk the rows, do not read them. The fourth is the one that tends to wake people up, because it is the only row where the answer today is that nobody is looking — a restricted license in a dependency is a legal exposure we are currently carrying without knowing. If someone asks whether stopping builds will slow delivery, the answer is that every check is off by default and we turn them on one at a time once we have seen what they would have caught.
