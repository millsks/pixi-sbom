What I'm not claiming

This is not a security product

It tells us what we ship. The tools that hunt for vulnerabilities still do that job, and now they get a complete list to work from.

It won't find what upstreams never published

Where a license or an advisory doesn't exist, the document records the gap rather than guessing. We'd rather show a known blank than a wrong answer.

It isn't a compliance program

It produces the evidence. Someone still has to own the process, and that ownership question is worth settling separately.

Put this in front of the objection rather than waiting for it. Executives have been sold too much and they discount anyone who won't name a limit. The third bullet is doing quiet work: it flags that adopting this raises a governance question we have not answered, which is better coming from you now than from an auditor later. If someone asks who should own it, say you have a view and will bring it with the results rather than deciding it here.
